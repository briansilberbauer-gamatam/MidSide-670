MidSide-670 v2.0.0 is the **studio-hardware redesign**: the same true Mid/Side
variable-mu compressor, rebuilt as a dark brushed-gunmetal desktop unit under a
flush, screwless glass panel — with a live waveform screen at its centre.

## Highlights

- **New brushed-gunmetal and glass interface.** A dimensional desktop unit sits
  on a shadowed studio surface, lit from the upper left. The chassis has fine
  horizontal brushed grain, chamfered matte edges, and a subtle muted-crimson
  LED edge-glow that refracts inward through the flush glass overlay. No
  visible screws or fasteners.
- **Live central waveform screen.** A real-time crimson trace over a dB/time
  grid, fed by lock-free min/max telemetry from the audio thread at roughly
  750 columns per second. It follows the true stereo envelope, so side-only and
  anti-phase material stays visible. Live MID/SIDE gain reduction is shown in
  the screen corner.
- **Hardware-grade controls.** Heavy-machined low-profile knobs with fine
  vertical knurling and a crimson under-glow, ultra-thin recessed glass
  sliders, and low-profile tactile buttons outlined by a thin red glow when
  active.
- **Preset pill readout.** The top-centre pill shows the current factory
  preset; step with `<` / `>` or click the name for the preset menu.
- **Scales cleanly.** A fixed 1180 × 720 design canvas scales with the window
  and preserves its aspect ratio from 885 × 540 up to 1770 × 1080.

## Compatibility

- **Same sound.** The DSP engine, parameter IDs and ranges, factory presets,
  and state layout are unchanged. Sessions and presets saved with v1.0.0 recall
  with identical settings and identical audio.
- Constant-latency, phase-aligned bypass is preserved.
- VST3 and Standalone builds are provided for **Windows x64**, **Linux x64**,
  and **macOS Universal** (`arm64` + `x86_64`). Every archive ships with a
  SHA-256 checksum.

## Install

- **Windows:** copy `MidSide-670.vst3` to `C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3\`.
- **macOS:** copy `MidSide-670.vst3` to `~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/`.
- **Linux:** copy `MidSide-670.vst3` to `~/.vst3/`.

The Standalone application is included in each archive. On macOS and Windows the
builds are unsigned/ad-hoc signed, so your OS may ask you to confirm the first
launch.
